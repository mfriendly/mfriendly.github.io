About

My research lies at the intersection of graph neural networks, time series forecasting, meta-learning, and epidemic modeling. I am broadly interested in how domain structure can be encoded as inductive biases for robust prediction across settings.

I am currently exploring applications in economics, energy and climate, and clinical domains, with an eye on causal and foundation-model directions.

Research Interests
  • Spatio-temporal graph neural networks (STGNNs)
  • Time series forecasting
  • Epidemic modeling and policy effect estimation
  • Meta-learning and graph structure learning
